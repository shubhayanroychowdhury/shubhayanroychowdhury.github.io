Research interests

Radar-based methods to study environmental change, flood processes and the Earth’s surface.

SAR

Synthetic Aperture Radar

Interpreting radar backscatter and polarization information to characterize land surfaces under different conditions.

FLOODS

Flood Mapping & Dynamics

Mapping inundation, monitoring flood evolution and investigating flood impacts on agricultural landscapes.

POLSAR

Radar Polarimetry

Developing dual-polarimetric descriptors to understand changes in radar scattering responses.

INSAR

Surface Deformation

Investigating ground displacement and terrain change using multitemporal interferometric SAR.

TIME SERIES

Temporal Earth Observation

Analysing multitemporal geospatial data to see how environmental signals evolve through time.

GEOAI

Geospatial Analytics

Integrating remote sensing, spatial analysis and computational methods for environmental applications.
