A Little About Me

Research at the intersection of radar and Earth science.

I am a PhD Research Scholar in Geoinformatics and Natural Resources Engineering at the Centre of Studies in Resources Engineering (CSRE), Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.

My research focuses on extracting meaningful information from microwave remote sensing observations. I am particularly interested in dual-polarimetric SAR, agricultural flood assessment, temporal flood dynamics and radar-based environmental monitoring.

Through the development of quantitative descriptors and reproducible geospatial workflows, I aim to improve how remotely sensed observations are translated into useful information for understanding natural hazards.

Turning radar observations into meaningful insights about a changing Earth.

Research Interests

Questions that drive my work.

Developing and applying radar-based methods to study environmental change, flood processes and the Earth's surface.

01 / SAR

Synthetic Aperture Radar

Interpreting radar backscatter and polarization information to characterize land surfaces under different environmental conditions.

02 / FLOODS

Flood Mapping & Dynamics

Mapping inundation, monitoring flood evolution and investigating the impacts of flooding on agricultural landscapes.

03 / POLSAR

Radar Polarimetry

Developing dual-polarimetric descriptors and quantitative approaches to understand changes in radar scattering responses.

04 / INSAR

Surface Deformation

Investigating ground displacement and terrain change using multitemporal interferometric SAR observations.

05 / TIME SERIES

Temporal Earth Observation

Analysing multitemporal geospatial data to explore how environmental signals evolve through time.

06 / GEOAI

Geospatial Analytics

Integrating remote sensing, spatial analysis and computational methods for environmental applications.

Selected Research

Projects & scientific contributions.

A selection of research directions spanning agricultural flood monitoring, radar-derived indices and ground deformation.

Published Research 01

Sentinel-1 Agricultural Flood Descriptor (SAFD)

A dual-polarimetric SAR-based approach for assessing flood-affected agricultural landscapes. The work explores radar-derived descriptors to distinguish flood impacts and support spatial agricultural flood assessment.

Ongoing Research 02

Dual-Polarimetric Agricultural Flood Index (DpAFI)

Research into a nonlinear transformation-based flood index using Sentinel-1 observations, with an emphasis on quantitative flood characterization and temporal variations in agricultural inundation.
